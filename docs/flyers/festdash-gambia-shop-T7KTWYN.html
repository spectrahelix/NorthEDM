How it works

  1. Scan the code and create your free account. Email or Google. It takes a minute and brings you straight to the shop set-up page, already linked to Gee.
  2. Tell us about your shop. What you sell, your area, your WhatsApp number, and the wallet where you want your money paid (Wave, QMoney or Afrimoney).
  3. Your coordinator confirms you. They visit, answer your questions, and collect the first month's listing fee. NorthEDM approves your shop.
  4. Add your items. A photo and a price in dalasi for each. Your shop goes live on FestDash.
  5. Customers order, a runner delivers, you get paid. People nearby order on the website or by WhatsApp and pay by Wave, QMoney, Afrimoney or cash on delivery. You hand over with a 4-digit code and keep 80% of every sale.

What it costs · what you earn

Listing fee
US$30 a month, paid in dalasi to your coordinator. No fee, no listing — stop any time.
Each sale
You keep 80%. 10% to your coordinator, 10% to NorthEDM.
Payouts
Your coordinator sends your share to the wallet you chose, on the schedule you agree with them.
