# Adding your first item — Gee's Shop

Takes about 3 minutes on your phone. Use Safari or Chrome.

**1. Open your inventory page**
northedm.com/vendor/dashboard — sign in with the same email you used before.
The first time, a short guide pops up and points at each box. Follow it, or
tap **Show me how to add an item** any time to see it again.

**2. Fill in the item**
- **Product name** — what a customer would look for (e.g. "Attaya tea set").
- **Price** — numbers only, in dalasi (e.g. 250).
- **In stock** — how many you have. Each sale counts one down.
- **Description** — one or two sentences.
- **Weight (g)** — optional, but it makes the item's spec sheet right.
- **Product specifications** — tap the chips (+ Brand, + Material, + Size…)
  and fill in the ones that apply. They show as a table on the item page.

**3. Add photos**
Tap **+ Add photos**, pick from your camera roll. The first photo is the cover.
Photos save the moment you add them.

**4. Publish**
Leave **Publish to my market** ticked and press **Add product**.

**5. Check it**
Tap **View my market →** (top right). Your item should be there. Your FestDash
menu and your store page (northedm.com/Gees) pick it up automatically.

**Also, one time only: your pickup spot**
Your shop is pickup-only, so customers need to know where to collect.
Open northedm.com/festdash/shop-setup, scroll to **Pickup location**, type where
you hand over (e.g. "Serrekunda market, stall 14, next to the mosque") and save.

**To change an item later**
Go back to northedm.com/vendor/dashboard, find it under **Products** and tap
**Edit**. Untick **Publish to my market** to hide it without deleting it.
